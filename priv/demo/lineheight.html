Line height

line-height: 1 — lines are packed tightly together, one line right after the other, so that descenders nearly touch the next line's ascenders.
normal — the built-in spacing, unchanged from before, with enough room between lines for easy reading of a paragraph of text.
line-height: 2 — double spaced lines, with the text centred in each line box so the extra space is split above and below.
line-height: 28px — a fixed pitch whatever the font size, even with big text inside the line.
