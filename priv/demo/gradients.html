Gradients

Linear gradients: a direction, an angle, colour stops with positions, and transparency.

Radial gradients: circle and ellipse, with a centre and hard stops.

Repeating linear gradients: stripes, a fade that repeats, a thin line pattern.

Repeating radial gradients: rings and a stretched ellipse.
